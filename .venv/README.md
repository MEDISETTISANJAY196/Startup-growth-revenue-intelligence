# Startup Growth & Revenue Intelligence Platform

## Project Overview

This project analyzes startup business data to understand revenue, customers, products, marketing performance, expenses, returns, and future revenue trends.

The objective is to convert raw business data into actionable insights that can help startups make data-driven decisions.

## Business Areas Analyzed

- Customer Analysis
- Sales Analysis
- Product Performance Analysis
- Marketing Performance Analysis
- Revenue Forecasting
- Operating Expense Analysis
- Refund and Return Analysis
- Final Business Insights

## Key Results

- Total Revenue: ₹50.36 Crore
- Total Orders: 60,000
- Total Customers: 10,000
- Total Products: 500
- Total Operating Expenses: ₹38.01 Crore
- Total Refund Loss: ₹4.91 Crore
- Final Profit: ₹7.44 Crore
- Profit Margin: 14.77%

## Key Insights

- Champions generated the highest customer revenue contribution.
- High-value At-Risk customers require retention strategies.
- Google Ads was the best-performing marketing channel.
- YouTube had the lowest marketing performance score.
- Star Products generated high revenue and strong profitability.
- Some products generated high revenue but low profit margins.
- Refunds created a significant financial impact.
- Revenue forecasting predicts approximately ₹2.63 Crore monthly revenue.
