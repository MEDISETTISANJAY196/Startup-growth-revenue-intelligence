# 📌 Project Overview

Startups generate large volumes of data from orders, customers, products, marketing campaigns, operational expenses, and product returns.

However, raw data alone does not help decision-makers understand:

- How much revenue is the business generating?
- Which products are most profitable?
- Which marketing channels perform best?
- How much money is lost due to refunds?
- What is the overall profit margin?
- What could future revenue look like?

The **Startup Growth & Revenue Intelligence Platform** transforms raw business data into meaningful insights and presents them through data analysis, visualizations, and an interactive Streamlit dashboard.

---

# 🎯 Problem Statement

Startups often have business data distributed across multiple areas such as sales, customers, products, marketing, expenses, and returns.

Without a centralized analysis system, it becomes difficult to identify:

- Revenue and profitability trends
- High-performing and underperforming products
- Effective marketing channels
- Customer segments contributing the most value
- Financial losses caused by refunds
- Future revenue trends

This project addresses that problem by analyzing business data and converting it into actionable insights that can support data-driven decision-making.

# 💡 Key Business Insights

- Champions generated the highest customer revenue contribution.
- High-value at-risk customers require retention strategies.
- Google Ads was the best-performing marketing channel.
- YouTube showed the lowest marketing performance score among the analyzed channels.
- Star Products generated strong revenue and profitability.
- Some products generated high revenue but had lower profit margins.
- Refunds created a significant financial impact on the business.
- The revenue forecast estimates average monthly revenue of approximately ₹2.63 Crore.
